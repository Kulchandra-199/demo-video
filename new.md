# Project NAGA
### Intralink Institutional Intelligence Platform  |  Board Brief  |  June 2025  |  Confidential

---

## The Opportunity

Over 13 years, Intralink has built something most firms cannot buy:

- Thousands of client engagements across Asia, Europe, and North America
- Deep relationships with stakeholders in Japan, Korea, China, and beyond
- Proven outcomes across semiconductors, robotics, manufacturing, and government relations
- A track record of market-entry projects that no competitor can replicate

**The problem is that almost none of it is accessible.**

It lives in Salesforce records that are incomplete. In emails no one searches. In meetings no one transcribed. In the heads of employees who may not be here in five years.

As Intralink grows, this becomes a structural risk — and a structural opportunity.

---

## What NAGA Is

**NAGA is Intralink's institutional intelligence platform.**

It captures, structures, and makes accessible everything the company knows — from 13 years of history and from every client interaction going forward.

It is not a chatbot. It is not a search tool. It is the intelligence layer that sits across Salesforce, Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, emails, and meetings — and turns fragmented information into actionable knowledge.

> The goal: every employee, on any engagement, immediately has access to what Intralink already knows.

---

## What It Solves

| Problem Today | What NAGA Delivers |
|---|---|
| Meeting outcomes not captured | Automatic summaries, action items, decisions, risks — synced to Salesforce |
| CRM data dependent on employee discipline | Automated updates from every call and email |
| New opportunities require research already done before | Historical pattern matching across 13 years of engagements |
| Expertise hard to find across the organisation | Expert and relationship discovery across the entire company |
| Key knowledge leaves when employees leave | Continuous capture into a permanent institutional record |
| Proposals take too long to develop | Relevant precedents, outcomes, and approaches surfaced instantly |

---

## Six Intelligence Domains

| Domain | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Communication Intelligence** | Turns meetings, emails, and Teams conversations into structured records — actions, decisions, risks, Salesforce updates |
| **Opportunity Intelligence** | Health scores, risk flags, next-best-action recommendations, and similar historical deals for every live opportunity |
| **Client Intelligence** | Complete view of every client — history, relationships, engagement activity, and strategic context |
| **Employee Intelligence** | Expertise discovery across the organisation — who has done what, where, with whom |
| **Market Entry Intelligence** | 13 years of Intralink market-entry experience made searchable — approaches, stakeholders, partnership models, outcomes by region and sector |
| **Outcome Intelligence** | Learns from every won deal, lost opportunity, and completed project to continuously sharpen recommendations |

---

## The Competitive Advantage Argument

Every competitor has access to industry reports, market research, and general AI tools.

**No competitor has access to Intralink's 13 years of accumulated experience.**

NAGA converts that experience into a durable, compounding advantage:

- Which approaches have worked for Japanese semiconductor companies entering Europe?
- Which stakeholders consistently influence robotics expansion decisions?
- Which partnership models have produced the strongest outcomes?

This is knowledge that cannot be purchased. It can only be accumulated over time — and Intralink has already accumulated it.

NAGA makes it usable.

## Rollout Plan

### Phase 1 — Communication Intelligence  *(Months 1–2)*
Automatic meeting summaries, action extraction, and Salesforce sync.
**Primary goal: eliminate manual administration.**

### Phase 2 — Opportunity Intelligence  *(Months 3–4)*
Deal health scoring, risk identification, next-best-action recommendations.
**Primary goal: improve win rates and forecasting accuracy.**

### Phase 3 — Employee and Client Intelligence  *(Months 5–6)*
Expertise discovery, relationship mapping, complete client history views.
**Primary goal: scale organisational knowledge across the business.**

### Phase 4 — Market Entry and Outcome Intelligence  *(Months 7–9)*
Historical pattern analysis, expansion recommendations, partnership intelligence.
**Primary goal: convert 13 years of experience into reusable business intelligence.**

### Phase 5 — Enterprise Intelligence Platform  *(Months 10–12)*
Unified intelligence layer across Salesforce, Microsoft 365, SharePoint, Teams, and all projects.
**Primary goal: Intralink's full institutional memory — live and continuously learning.**

---

## Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Data quality of historical records | Phase 1 establishes clean capture going forward; historical enrichment is incremental |
| Employee adoption | Phase 1 starts with passive capture — no behaviour change required from employees |
| Salesforce data sensitivity | Role-based access controls; NAGA respects existing Salesforce permissions |
| AI accuracy on client-sensitive outputs | All AI outputs are surfaced as drafts or intelligence — no automatic commits without review |
| Scope creep across five phases | Each phase has a defined primary goal and discrete deliverable; board reviews between phases |

---


Phase 1 delivers measurable operational value within 60 days and validates the platform before further investment is committed.

The build is scoped. The rollout is phased. The risk is contained.

**The only question is how much longer that knowledge stays locked away.**
