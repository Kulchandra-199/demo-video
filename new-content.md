# PROJECT NAGA

## Intralink Institutional Intelligence Platform



---

# Executive Summary

Over 35 years, Intralink has accumulated a unique body of knowledge, relationships, market-entry experience, and client intelligence across Asia, Europe, and North America.

This knowledge exists across Salesforce, Microsoft 365, Teams, SharePoint, emails, documents, project records, and the experience of employees throughout the organisation.

Today, much of that knowledge remains fragmented and difficult to access.

Project NAGA is a long-term initiative to create a unified intelligence platform that enables every employee to access organisational knowledge, AI capabilities, and business systems through a single interface.

The vision is not simply to deploy AI tools. It is to create a platform that captures institutional knowledge, improves productivity, automates routine work, and ultimately becomes Intralink's operational intelligence layer.

---

# The Opportunity

Intralink possesses assets that competitors cannot easily replicate:

• 35 years of market-entry experience

• Thousands of client engagements

• Deep relationships across Japan, Korea, China, Europe, and North America

• Expertise across semiconductors, robotics, manufacturing, government relations, and technology sectors

• Proven delivery methodologies and commercial outcomes

While this experience exists throughout the organisation, much of it is not easily discoverable or reusable.

As Intralink continues to grow, the ability to capture, organise, and leverage this knowledge becomes increasingly important.

NAGA aims to transform this accumulated experience into a scalable organisational asset.

---

# Vision

NAGA will become Intralink's unified AI and intelligence platform.

Employees will have a single destination where they can:

• Access leading AI models

• Search organisational knowledge

• Interact with company systems

• Automate repetitive tasks

• Retrieve information from meetings, emails, and documents

• Access historical expertise and project knowledge

• Receive assistance tailored to their role and responsibilities

Over time, NAGA will evolve from an AI access platform into an intelligent operational assistant integrated throughout the business.

---

# Strategic Objectives

The programme has four primary objectives:

## 1. Increase Productivity

Provide employees with access to advanced AI capabilities through a secure company platform.

## 2. Improve Knowledge Accessibility

Make organisational information easier to discover and utilise.

## 3. Reduce Administrative Burden

Automate routine activities such as note-taking, information retrieval, summarisation, and data updates.

## 4. Build Long-Term Institutional Intelligence

Create a continuously growing knowledge asset that compounds in value over time.

---

# Phase 1 – Foundation Platform

## Objective

Establish the foundation for NAGA while gathering requirements, validating use cases, and driving user adoption.

## Deliverables

### Unified AI Portal

The first NAGA product will be a secure internal portal that provides employees with access to multiple leading AI models through a single interface.

Rather than forcing users into a single model, employees will be able to select the model best suited to their needs, including platforms such as ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and future approved models.

Typical use cases include:

• Writing and drafting

• Research and analysis

• Translation

• Summarisation

• Meeting preparation

• Content generation

• Market intelligence

### Dedicated Productivity Tools

The platform will provide specialised experiences for common business activities, including:

• Translation workflows

• Document summarisation

• Research assistance

• Content creation

• Internal knowledge search

### NAGA Personal Assistant (Initial Version)

Phase 1 will also establish the first version of the NAGA Personal Assistant.

Initially, the assistant will focus on:

• Conversational interaction

• Information retrieval

• Organisational search

• User support

The purpose of this stage is to establish the framework that future capabilities will build upon.

### Requirements Discovery

A major goal of Phase 1 is understanding how employees naturally use AI and identifying the highest-value opportunities for future automation and intelligence capabilities.

Usage patterns and feedback will directly inform later development phases.

## Success Criteria

• High user adoption

• Positive employee feedback

• Identification of priority business workflows

• Validation of platform architecture

• Defined roadmap for subsequent phases

# Key Risks and Mitigations

## User Adoption

Mitigation:
Focus initial delivery on immediate employee value and productivity improvements.

## Data Governance

Mitigation:
Implement role-based access controls and align with existing security policies.

## AI Accuracy

Mitigation:
Maintain human review for business-critical outputs and decisions.

## Scope Management

Mitigation:
Deliver through clearly defined phases with executive review at each stage.

## Integration Complexity

Mitigation:
Prioritise high-value integrations and adopt an incremental deployment approach.

# Closing

NAGA is not a single AI tool.

It is a long-term platform designed to provide every employee with access to AI capabilities, organisational knowledge, and business systems through a unified experience.

The first phase focuses on establishing this foundation, driving adoption, and understanding where AI can create the greatest value across the business.

Over time, NAGA has the potential to become a strategic asset that captures, preserves, and amplifies the collective intelligence of Intralink.

The objective is simple:

To ensure that the knowledge, experience, and expertise accumulated over 35 years becomes accessible to every employee and continues to create value for decades to come.
