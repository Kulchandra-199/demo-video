## Notes for Voice Recording

### Tone Guidelines
- **Professional yet approachable** — confident but not arrogant
- **Clear and measured pace** — not rushed, allowing key points to land
- **Slight warmth** — this is a trusted colleague, not a sales pitch
- **Emphasis on key phrases:** "35 years", "cannot be purchased", "structural opportunity"

### Timing Notes
- Total runtime: ~3 minutes 15 seconds
- Allow 0.5–1 second pause between major sections
- Speed up slightly on list items (Scene 3, 4, 6)
- Slow down on key messages (Scene 5 competitive advantage, Scene 9 closing)

### Pronunciation Guide
- **NAGA:** "NAY-guh" (hard G as in "get")
- **Intralink:** "IN-truh-link"
- **Salesforce:** "SALES-force"
